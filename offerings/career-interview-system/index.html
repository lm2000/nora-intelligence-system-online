Clear roles, one preparation system

Nora AI Lab organizes the system. The candidate leads the conversation.

Nora AI Lab makes preparation coordinated and reusable. It does not substitute for the candidate's experience, judgment, voice, or the interview itself.

What Nora AI Lab adds

  • A connected view of the opportunity, role, stage, and preparation material
  • A stage-specific preparation package: research, stories, questions, drills, and decision points
  • Structured rehearsal and concise delivery feedback
  • Reusable learning from interviews, mocks, and practice

What the candidate leads

  • The facts, stories, and experience that are true to them
  • What to emphasize, revise, or leave out
  • Every answer, decision, and employer conversation
  • The final judgment about what is useful next

The part that compounds

Each conversation leaves a useful trace for the next one.

Instead of scattered notes, old reports, and one-off practice, the system retains the preparation, the event, and the useful feedback as one evolving career asset.

A focused starting point

Start with the next important stage—and keep the preparation that earned it.

The point is not to automate a person. It is to give an experienced candidate a durable way to prepare, decide, rehearse, and improve across the conversations that matter.
