Clear roles, one coordinated workflow

AI coordinates the work. The agency keeps authority.

Nora AI Lab adds depth and preparation. Boutique Marketing Agency retains the judgment, relationship leadership, and final decisions that make the work valuable.

What Nora AI Lab adds

  • Market, competitor, and account research
  • Buyer and buying-committee context
  • Account-specific value hypotheses and message options
  • Structured feedback that improves the next package

What the agency leads

  • Target selection and commercial judgment
  • Client relationships and brand voice
  • Review, revision, and final approval
  • The client conversation itself
