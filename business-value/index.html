The coordination gap

Organizations have AI tools. What they lack is a reliable way to make them work together.

Outputs are easy. Durable execution is harder. Nora focuses on the operating layer between a business decision and everything that must happen afterward.

01

Scattered context

Knowledge lives across people, documents, inboxes, systems, and one-off AI conversations.

02

Disconnected execution

Strategy, recommendations, approvals, and operational follow-through rarely share one durable workflow.

03

Weak accountability

Leaders struggle to trace what happened, why it happened, who approved it, and what was learned.
